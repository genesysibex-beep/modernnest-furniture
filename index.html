Built for the long haul

ModernNest started with a simple frustration: most furniture is designed to be replaced, not repaired. We work with small workshops that build with kiln-dried hardwood, mortise-and-tenon joints, and finishes that age gracefully instead of wearing out.

Every piece ships with a care guide, and every workshop we partner with stands behind their work with a genuine repair service — not just a replacement policy.
